Free Online Audio Cutter

PDFreeto's Audio Cutter lets you trim any audio file directly in your browser — no upload, no account, no waiting. Your audio file never leaves your device. Load the file, drag to select your clip on the waveform, and download.

Supported formats

The Audio Cutter accepts any format your browser can decode: MP3, WAV, OGG, M4A, AAC, FLAC, WebM and OPUS. The output is a high-quality WAV file that preserves the original audio quality of your selection.

How to trim audio

Upload your file by dropping it on the page or clicking to browse. The waveform loads automatically. Click and drag on the waveform to select the section you want to keep. Type precise start and end times in the fields below the waveform for exact cuts. Click Play Selection to preview your clip before downloading.

Privacy

The entire operation runs in your browser using the Web Audio API. Your audio file is processed locally — no data is sent to any server at any point.
